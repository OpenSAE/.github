## Mission

**Understand intelligence. Expand our capacity to understand it.**

OpenSAE is an open research initiative advancing mechanistic interpretability through scientific research, open-source infrastructure, and collaborative AI.

We study how neural networks represent knowledge, how learned concepts interact, and how internal mechanisms give rise to intelligent behavior. We build the tools that enable researchers and AI agents to investigate these questions together.

Our ambition is to make mechanistic interpretability more accessible, scalable, and collaborative.

## Vision

**Every discovery should empower the next.**

The mechanistic interpretability community has accumulated a remarkable collection of research methods, models, datasets, and scientific insights. AI agents present an unprecedented opportunity to connect these resources and transform them into reusable research capabilities.

We envision a future where researchers and AI agents can build upon the collective knowledge of the interpretability community, combine existing methods to explore new hypotheses, and conduct increasingly ambitious scientific investigations.

Every researcher should have access to the research capabilities developed by the community.

## Open Source Projects

### [SAEBase](https://github.com/OpenSAE/SAEBase)

**An open research platform for understanding language models.**

SAEBase connects mechanistic interpretability methods, feature interventions, model evaluation, and reproducible experiments within a unified research platform.

By transforming individual research methods into reusable components, SAEBase enables researchers and AI agents to combine existing techniques, investigate complex model behaviors, and explore new scientific hypotheses at scale.

Our goal is to turn the growing body of interpretability research into capabilities that the entire community can build upon.

[GitHub](https://github.com/OpenSAE/SAEBase) · [Research Lab](https://huggingface.co/spaces/OpenSAE/SAEBase) · [Evaluation Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenSAE/SAEBase-Evaluation)

### [EverResearch](https://github.com/OpenSAE/EverResearch)

**Research infrastructure for the agentic AI era.**

EverResearch enables AI agents to understand, organize, maintain, and extend complex research projects. It connects scientific knowledge, reusable code, experimental workflows, and research results into a continuously evolving research environment.

Supporting Codex, Claude, Cursor, Gemini, DeepSeek, and other agent ecosystems, EverResearch brings automated research workflows to scientists across different AI platforms.

Built from our experience in AI research, EverResearch extends our vision of collaborative scientific discovery beyond mechanistic interpretability.

[GitHub](https://github.com/OpenSAE/EverResearch) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/ever-research/) · [Agent Skills](https://skills.sh/opensae/everresearch)

## Research

**From individual features to the mechanisms of intelligence.**

Our research investigates fundamental properties of learned representations and their relationship to model behavior.

- 🧩 [**Behavioral Compositionality of Sparse Autoencoder Representations**](https://github.com/OpenSAE/SAE-Behavioral-Compositionality) — Understanding how feature interactions jointly shape language model behavior.
- 🔀 [**Representation Redistribution in Sparse Autoencoders**](https://github.com/OpenSAE/Redistribution-Sensitivity-Score) — Revealing semantic redistribution among learned feature concepts across contextual variations.
- 🧭 [**Sparse Autoencoders Do Not Guarantee Local Features**](https://github.com/OpenSAE/SAE-Input-Localization) — Investigating the theoretical foundations and limitations of feature localization.
