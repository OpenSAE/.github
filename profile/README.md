## Mission

OpenSAE advances the science of how neural networks represent information and how their internal structures shape behavior. We develop theory, methods, and open research tools to make explanations testable and interventions reproducible.

Sparse autoencoders are a central starting point, connecting our work to broader questions in mechanistic interpretability and representation learning.

## Research Directions

| Direction | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Representation Structure** | What neural networks represent and how features are organized. |
| **Behavior and Intervention** | How internal components and their interactions influence behavior. |
| **Context and Generalization** | How representations change or persist across contexts. |
| **Evaluation and Reliability** | How to rigorously test explanations and interventions. |

## Research & Infrastructure

| Project | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| **[SAEBase](https://github.com/OpenSAE/SAEBase)** | Open infrastructure for SAE evaluation, feature interventions, and reproducible research. |
| **[SAR — Behavioral Compositionality](https://github.com/OpenSAE/SAE-Behavioral-Compositionality)** | Understanding how feature interactions shape model behavior. |
| **[RSS — Representation Redistribution](https://github.com/OpenSAE/Redistribution-Sensitivity-Score)** | Studying structured changes in representations across contexts. |

## Open Science

We share research, code, evaluation protocols, and tools to support an open, rigorous, and reproducible understanding of neural networks.
