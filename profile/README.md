## Mission

**Understand intelligence. Accelerate discovery.**

OpenSAE is an open research initiative advancing mechanistic interpretability, agentic AI, and collaborative scientific discovery.

We develop research infrastructure that enables humans and AI agents to understand complex systems, conduct reproducible experiments, and build upon existing scientific knowledge.

Our goal is to make powerful research capabilities accessible to every scientist.

## Vision

**A future where scientific progress compounds.**

Every research project produces valuable knowledge, methods, code, and experimental evidence. Much of this knowledge remains difficult to understand, reproduce, and reuse.

We envision a scientific ecosystem where research capabilities accumulate across projects, discoveries become reusable foundations for future work, and AI agents help researchers explore questions that were previously too complex or expensive to investigate.

Scientists should be free to focus on the questions that matter most.

## Open Source Projects

### [EverResearch](https://github.com/OpenSAE/EverResearch)

**Research infrastructure for the agentic AI era.**

EverResearch enables AI agents to understand, organize, maintain, and extend scientific projects. It connects research code, experiments, configurations, and knowledge through reusable research workflows.

With support for Codex, Claude, Cursor, Gemini, DeepSeek, and other agent ecosystems, EverResearch aims to make continuous, reproducible scientific research accessible across AI platforms.

[GitHub](https://github.com/OpenSAE/EverResearch) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/ever-research/) · [Agent Skills](https://skills.sh/opensae/everresearch)

### [SAEBase](https://github.com/OpenSAE/SAEBase)

**From understanding individual features to understanding intelligence.**

SAEBase is an open platform for mechanistic interpretability, connecting model representations, feature interventions, and large-scale LLM evaluation.

By turning research methods into reusable components, SAEBase enables researchers and AI agents to combine existing techniques, investigate model behavior, and explore new scientific hypotheses.

[GitHub](https://github.com/OpenSAE/SAEBase) · [Research Lab](https://huggingface.co/spaces/OpenSAE/SAEBase) · [Evaluation Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenSAE/SAEBase-Evaluation)

## Research

**From interpreting features to understanding intelligent systems.**

Our research investigates how neural networks represent information, how learned concepts interact, and how internal representations shape model behavior.

- [**Behavioral Compositionality of Sparse Autoencoder Representations**](https://github.com/OpenSAE/SAE-Behavioral-Compositionality) — Understanding how feature interactions jointly shape model behavior.
- [**Representation Redistribution in Sparse Autoencoders**](https://github.com/OpenSAE/Redistribution-Sensitivity-Score) — Understanding how semantic information is redistributed across features under contextual variation.
- [**Sparse Autoencoders Do Not Guarantee Local Features**](https://github.com/OpenSAE/SAE-Input-Localization) — Investigating the fundamental properties and limitations of learned representations.
