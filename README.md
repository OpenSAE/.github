## Mission

OpenSAE studies how learned representations shape the behavior of AI systems. We develop theory, methods, and open research tools to characterize feature structure, measure behavioral effects, understand interactions, and evaluate explanations across contexts.

Sparse autoencoders (SAEs) provide a central setting for this work. Our research also connects to broader questions in mechanistic interpretability, representation learning, and model evaluation.

## Research Directions

| Direction | Research focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Representation Structure** | Characterize learned features, their input dependencies, and the structure of internal representations. |
| **Behavior and Intervention** | Measure how individual features and their interactions influence downstream model behavior. |
| **Context and Generalization** | Study how representations change across prompts and environments, including stability and redistribution. |
| **Evaluation and Reliability** | Develop quantitative measures, controlled tests, and theoretical foundations for faithful explanations. |

Our approach combines mathematical analysis, controlled interventions, behavioral evaluation, and reproducible experimentation.

## Open Science

We share research methods, reproducible implementations, evaluation protocols, and tutorials to support rigorous study of learned representations. Clear definitions, systematic baselines, and controlled experiments guide our work.
