# OpenSAE Code of Conduct

OpenSAE welcomes people with different levels of experience and different research perspectives. Technical disagreement is expected; personal hostility is not.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss code, claims, methods, and evidence respectfully and specifically.
- Give useful feedback and credit others' contributions.
- Be patient with questions and good-faith mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing private conversations or credentials.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, personal attacks, threats, persistent disruption, and sharing someone's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

If a community interaction violates these standards, email [hma232@wisc.edu](mailto:hma232@wisc.edu) with the subject “OpenSAE conduct report”. Provide relevant links and context. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

The project maintainer reviews reports and may remove comments, close threads, request changes in behavior, or restrict participation when appropriate. Reports will be handled with care, without promising anonymity or response times that cannot be guaranteed.

These expectations apply to the OpenSAE GitHub organization and project contribution channels.
